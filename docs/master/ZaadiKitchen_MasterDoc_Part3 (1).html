Admin Portal — C6 to C10
Continues from Part 2 (C1–C5). Covers the Revenue Dashboard, Menu Manager, Customer Management, Comms & Automations, and Delivery Areas & Buildings.
C6
Revenue Dashboard
A live financial overview of the business. Accessible to the Administrator role only. Shows MRR, subscriber counts, daily revenue trends, plan distribution, and key performance metrics.
MRR Card
MRR figure
Monthly Recurring Revenue — the total value of all active subscriptions in the current billing cycle. Shown as a large SAR figure at the top of the screen.
% change
Percentage change versus the prior full month (not month-to-date). Shown as a green badge with an up arrow when positive.
Subscriber Stats
Active
Current active subscribers
All customers with a live, non-expired, non-paused subscription. Tapping routes to Customer Management with the "Active" filter applied.
New Today
Signed up today
Customers whose first subscription activated today. Tapping routes to Customer Management filtered to "Joined Today."
Churned
Lapsed without renewal
Customers whose plan expired and who did not renew within 3 days. Tapping routes to Customer Management with the "Churned" filter applied.
📌
Churn definition
A customer is counted as churned when their plan expires and they have not renewed within 3 days of the expiry date. Customers who are paused are not counted as churned — they are still active. Customers who cancelled their plan but whose plan has not yet ended are also not counted as churned until the end date passes.
Daily Revenue Bar Chart
X-axis
Days of the current week (Sunday–Thursday). The chart reflects the Saudi working week.
Current day
Highlighted with a full mint-green bar. The label below the bar is shown in mint and bold.
Future days
Shown as faded, lighter bars — indicating projected or expected revenue based on active subscriptions. Not yet confirmed revenue.
Period selector
A dropdown in the top-right of the chart allows switching between the current week, the current month, and previous periods.
Subscribers by Plan

Four horizontal bar rows showing the distribution of active subscribers across plan types: Month Plan · Weekly Plan · Quarterly Plan · Try It. Each bar is proportional to the total active subscriber count. The exact count is shown to the right of each bar.

Tapping any plan bar routes to the Customer Management screen with that plan type filter applied — allowing the admin to see exactly which customers are on each plan.

Key Metrics (KPI Cards)
Avg Skip Rate
The average number of skips per active subscriber within their current billing cycle. A rising skip rate can indicate low meal satisfaction or over-subscription. Tapping shows a breakdown by plan type.
Salad Meal %
The percentage of active subscribers on the Salad Meal type versus Executive Meal. Useful for kitchen production planning. Not the same as the Salad Add-On — that feature is not in v1.
Salad Attach Rate
Not in v1. This KPI card does not appear on the Revenue Dashboard. The Salad Add-On (+SAR 15 daily item) has not been launched.
C7
Menu Manager
Where the Administrator plans and publishes the weekly meal menu. Two tabs: Week Planner (assigning meals to each day) and Meal Library (managing the catalogue of available dishes). Administrator access only.
Week Planner Tab
The grid
A 5-day grid showing Sunday through Thursday for the selected week. Each day can have one Executive Meal and one Salad Meal assigned. Days with a meal show the dish name and kcal. Empty days show a "+ Add meal" placeholder.
Week navigation
‹ and › arrows allow the admin to move between weeks. The admin can plan up to 4 weeks ahead. Past weeks are read-only — the edit and star buttons are greyed out and cannot be tapped.
Edit (✏️)
Shown on each assigned meal slot. Tapping opens the Assign Dish Picker for that day/slot — allowing the admin to swap the assigned dish for a different one from the library.
Star (⭐)
Marks a dish as a recurring favourite. Starred dishes appear at the top of the Meal Library and the Assign Dish Picker list, making frequently used dishes quicker to assign.
Publish button
The "📅 Publish Week's Menu" CTA is disabled until all 5 days have both a meal assigned. Once all slots are filled the button activates. After publishing, the week's menu becomes visible in the customer app immediately. Published weeks are locked — individual meal slots cannot be swapped after publishing.
Unpublished weeks
A yellow info banner is shown when days remain empty: "X days still need meals assigned before you can publish."
Meal Library Tab
What it shows
All dishes ever added to the system, regardless of which week they are assigned to. Each row shows: emoji or photo thumbnail · meal name · Arabic name · kcal · protein · Active or Draft status badge · Edit button.
Active status
Meal is available for assignment to any week. Shown in full opacity.
Draft status
Meal has been added but not yet activated. Shown at 50% opacity in the library list. Cannot be assigned to a week until it is activated. A Draft meal must be manually changed to Active status before it can appear in the Assign Dish Picker.
Starred meals
Appear at the top of the library list with a star icon. All other active meals appear below in alphabetical order.
Filter chips
All · Executive · Salad · Chicken · Lamb (and other protein types). Chips filter the library list to the selected category.
Adding a New Dish
XLSX upload
The admin can upload a spreadsheet to bulk-import dish data. The XLSX import populates: meal name, meal type (Executive/Salad), calories, protein, carbs, fat, and Chef's Note. Image upload is always manual — it cannot be done via the spreadsheet.
Manual entry
All fields can be entered manually: meal type toggle · English name · nutrition grid (calories, protein, carbs, fat) · Chef's Note · photo upload · emoji picker (if no photo).
Photo upload
JPG or PNG, maximum 5MB. If a photo is uploaded, it is shown in the customer app as the meal's hero image. If no photo is uploaded, the selected emoji is shown on a colour-coded gradient background (orange for Executive, green for Salad).
Save behaviour
All new dishes are saved as Draft regardless of how they were added. The admin must manually change the status to Active in the Meal Library before the dish can be assigned to a week.
Assign Dish Picker

A modal that opens when tapping an empty "+ Add meal" slot or the ✏️ Edit button on an assigned slot. Shows the Meal Library list filtered to the relevant meal type (Executive or Salad) for that slot.

A search field at the top filters the list as the admin types. Each row shows the dish thumbnail, English name, kcal, and protein.

Already-used meals are greyed out and show a "Used — [Day]" label indicating they are already assigned to another day in the current week. This prevents the same dish appearing twice in one week.

The "✓ Assign [Dish Name]" CTA at the bottom becomes active when a dish is selected. Tapping it assigns the dish and closes the picker.

C8
Customer Management
A searchable, filterable list of all customers. Tapping a customer row expands an inline detail view — there is no separate customer detail screen. Administrator access only.
Filter Chips

Eight filter chips at the top of the screen allow the admin to segment the list. These filter states mirror the drilldown destinations from the Revenue Dashboard — tapping a stat on the Revenue Dashboard opens Customer Management pre-filtered.

Status filters
All · Active · Joined Today · Churned
Status-based filters. "Joined Today" shows customers whose first plan activated today. "Churned" matches the Revenue Dashboard definition: expired + no renewal within 3 days.
Plan type filters
Month · Week · Quarterly · Try It
Filters to customers currently on that plan type. These match the plan bar drilldowns on the Revenue Dashboard.
Customer List Rows
Each row shows
Avatar initial (coloured circle) · Full name · Plan type badge (colour-coded: Month = mint, Week = lemon, Quarterly = purple, Try It = papaya) · Status chip (Active / Paused / Expired) · Remaining days or expiry note.
Tapping a row
Expands the row inline — no navigation to a new screen. The row gains a mint left border and the detail area appears directly below the customer's row in the list.
Churned customers
Shown at reduced opacity (60%) to visually distinguish them from active customers. Still tappable for detail.
Inline Expanded Detail
Plan
Plan name · meal type · plan end date.
Wallet
Current wallet balance in SAR.
Address
Building name and floor/desk. Read-only.
Issues
Count of past issues submitted by this customer and their resolution status (e.g. "1 past · All resolved").
Actions
Three action buttons: 📞 Contact (opens a WhatsApp link to the customer's phone number) · 📋 View history (opens the customer's full order and issue history) · 🗒️ Add note (attaches an internal admin note to this customer record, not visible to the customer).
C9
Comms & Automations
Where automated customer notifications are managed and broadcast messages are sent. Administrator access only. All communications in v1 are delivered as push notifications — not WhatsApp or SMS.
All Communications Are Push Notifications
🔔
Every automated message and broadcast is a push notification
In v1, all customer communications from the Comms & Automations screen are sent as push notifications. WhatsApp and SMS are not used for any automated messaging — they are reserved exclusively for OTP verification at sign-in. This applies to automations and broadcast messages alike.
Automations Tab — 5 Toggles
1. Delivery Confirmed ✅
Trigger: Driver taps "Mark as Delivered" in the Rider App for a specific customer's order.
Message: Push notification to that customer confirming their meal has arrived at their desk.
Default state: On.
2. End-of-Day Feedback ⭐
Trigger: 3:00 PM daily, sent to every customer who received a meal that day.
Message: Push notification asking the customer to rate their meal. Includes the meal name. Shows "Rate now →" and "Later" action buttons.
Default state: On.
3. Renewal Reminder 🔄
Trigger: 48 hours before a customer's plan end date.
Message: Push notification reminding the customer their plan is ending and inviting them to renew.
Default state: On.
4. Referral Reward 🎁
Trigger: A referred friend's plan activates (not when they pay — when the plan goes live).
Message: Push notification to the referrer confirming their wallet credit. Shows the credit amount (10% of the friend's plan price) and their new wallet balance.
Default state: On.
5. Lapsed Reactivation 😴
Trigger: A customer's plan has expired and they have not renewed within 3 days.
Message: Push notification inviting the lapsed customer to come back and renew.
Default state: Off (admin must activate).
Broadcast Tab
Audience segment
Five segment chips: All active · Month plan · Lapsed · Delivering Today · Custom. Selecting a chip updates the "To: N subscribers" count below it in real time.
Delivering Today segment
Targets all customers who have a meal being delivered on the current day. Useful for same-day operational messages such as a delivery delay notice or a menu change announcement.
Custom segment
Allows the admin to define a more specific audience — by plan type, area, meal type, or other criteria. The exact custom filter UI is defined in a future iteration.
Message composer
Free text area. Character count shown (e.g. "142 / 320 characters"). No formatting or images in v1 — plain text only.
Send button
Shows the audience count: "📢 Send to N subscribers." Sends the message as a push notification to all devices in the selected segment immediately.
Channel
Push notification. Not WhatsApp. Not SMS.
C10
Delivery Areas & Buildings
Where the admin manages delivery coverage. Areas populate the customer-facing Area Search dropdown during onboarding. Buildings within each area populate the building auto-suggest. Administrator access only.
Areas and Zones — Same Thing
📍
Area = Zone — there is no separate zone level
The data structure is flat: Areas sit at the top level, and Buildings sit within each area. There is no separate "Zone" entity above Areas. When customers search for their area during onboarding, they are selecting from this list of Areas directly.
Area Status Types
Active
The area is live. Customers can select it during onboarding and sign up for deliveries. Shown with a green dot in the area list.
Coming Soon
The area is visible to customers in the Area Search dropdown but cannot be selected. Customers see it as a future option — useful for generating interest and capturing demand before launch. Shown with a gold dot.
Paused
The area is completely hidden from all customers. Existing customers in this area are not affected but no new sign-ups are possible. Shown with an orange dot.
Area Fields
Area name
Required. The name customers see in the Area Search dropdown during onboarding. Example: "Al Nakheel", "Olaya Business District."
Coverage description
Internal description of the geographic boundary. Example: "King Fahad Rd corridor, north of Olaya intersection." Not shown to customers.
Distance from kitchen
In kilometres. Shown in the customer Area Search dropdown to help them understand delivery timing.
Est. delivery time
Approximate delivery time for this area. Shown in the Area Search dropdown.
Launch status
Active / Coming Soon / Paused. Radio selector with descriptions of what each status means for customer visibility.
Planned launch date
Only shown when status is "Coming Soon." The intended go-live date. For internal planning — not currently displayed to customers.
Internal notes
Free text for the admin team. Example: "Needs extra rider. Coordinate with ops before May 1." Never shown to customers.
Activating an Area — Status Change Warning
⚠️
Switching to Active opens signups immediately
When an admin changes an area's status to Active (whether from Coming Soon or Paused), a warning block is shown: "Switching to Active immediately allows new customers to select this area and sign up. Make sure your rider capacity and building list are ready before activating." The admin must then tap the "✓ Activate Area" button to confirm. This warning exists to prevent premature activation before the operations team is ready.
Buildings Within an Area
What buildings are
The specific office buildings within a delivery area. These populate the building auto-suggest field that customers see during onboarding when filling in their Building Details. Only buildings added by the admin appear in the auto-suggest.
Building fields
Building name only. There are no other fields — no address, no floor count, no capacity. The name is what matters: it must be the recognisable name as it appears on the building or in maps. Example: "Al Nakheel Tower, King Fahad Rd."
Adding buildings
From the Building List screen for a specific area: tap "+ Add Building to [Area Name]." Enter the building name and tap Save. The building is immediately available in the customer auto-suggest for that area.
Editing buildings
Each building row has an ✏️ Edit button. Opens the Edit Building form with the name pre-filled. Save updates the name across all existing customer address records that reference this building.
Deleting buildings
A Delete button is available in the Edit Building form. Deleting removes the building from the auto-suggest so new customers cannot select it. Existing customer addresses that reference this building are not affected — their delivery address continues to show the building name correctly.
Customer free text
Customers can type a building name that is not in the admin list. This free text is accepted and used for their delivery — but it is never stored in the building list and will not appear as a suggestion for any other customer. Only the admin controls what appears in the auto-suggest.
D
Rider Application
The Driver role's interface. A focused, single-purpose app showing today's delivery list. No customer data, no financial information, no admin access. Accessible after selecting the Driver role at login.
D1
My Deliveries
The driver's daily delivery list. Shows all orders assigned to this rider for today, in sequence order. The driver marks each delivery as complete using a button — no QR code in v1.
Screen Header
Title
"My Deliveries" with the current date and the driver's name. Example: "My Deliveries · Thu, Apr 3 · Khalid 🛵".
Progress counter
Shows completed vs total deliveries for the day — e.g. "5/87." This counter increments each time the driver taps "Mark as Delivered." It is a running total for the rider's own reference.
Stats row
Three figures: Done (completed deliveries) · Remaining (not yet delivered) · Issues (flagged deliveries). Each updates in real time as the driver works through the list.
Area Filter

A horizontal filter bar below the header allows the driver to filter the delivery list by area. Options: All Areas (default) · then one chip per active delivery area (e.g. "Al Nakheel" · "Olaya" · "Al Malaz").

This is useful when a single rider covers multiple areas in sequence — they can filter to one area at a time to work through that area's deliveries before moving to the next.

Delivery Row States
Completed
Green background. Shows: sequence number (filled mint circle with ✓) · customer name · building and floor · meal type · timestamp ("Delivered at 12:32 PM"). No action buttons — read-only. Greyed out visually to distinguish from active deliveries.
Active (current)
White background with a mint left border to highlight it as the current delivery. Shows: sequence number · customer name · full address (building, floor, desk) · meal type · delivery preference (Hand to me / Leave at reception). Two elements: a full-width green "✅ Mark as Delivered" button and a small ⚠️ flag icon in the top-right corner of the row.
Pending
White background, no accent. Same content as the active row but without the Mark as Delivered button visible at full prominence. The ⚠️ flag icon is available. These deliveries have not yet been reached in the sequence.
Mark as Delivered
✅
No QR code — button only
Delivery confirmation is done by tapping the "✅ Mark as Delivered" button on the delivery row. There is no QR code, no camera scan, and no NFC in v1. On tap: (1) The delivery row transitions to the Completed state. (2) A timestamp is recorded. (3) The progress counter increments. (4) A push notification is sent to the customer confirming their meal has arrived.
Delivery Preference

Each delivery row shows the customer's delivery preference: "Hand to me" or "Leave at reception." This is set by the customer in their account settings and cannot be changed by the rider. It tells the rider exactly what to do on arrival — whether to wait for the customer personally or leave the meal at the reception desk.

D2
Issue Report (Driver)
When a delivery cannot be completed normally, the driver taps the ⚠️ flag icon on that row to file an issue report. The report is pre-filled with the customer and meal details — the driver only needs to select a reason and optionally add notes.
Issue Report Form
Pre-filled context
The customer's name, meal type, and delivery address are automatically filled from the delivery row. The driver does not need to type any of this — it is read-only context.
Reason chips
Single-select chips. Four options: Customer not found · Wrong address · Damaged packaging · Other. One must be selected before the form can be submitted.
Notes field
Optional free text. For example: "Called customer twice, no answer. Left at reception per their preference." Provides additional context to the ops team reviewing the issue.
Submit
Creates an issue record in the admin Daily Ops queue immediately. The Issues counter in the driver's header stats increments by 1. The delivery row remains in its current state — the driver can attempt re-delivery or move on based on their instructions.
Cancel
Returns to the delivery list without filing a report. No record is created.
What Happens After the Driver Files a Report
Driver taps ⚠️ flag
→
Selects reason, adds notes
→
Submits report
→
Issue appears in admin Daily Ops queue
→
Admin resolves: Credit / Escalate / Reject
The driver does not see the outcome of the issue resolution — that is handled entirely in the admin portal. The driver's role ends at submission.
E
Cross-Cutting Rules
Rules and constraints that apply across multiple features and roles. Read this section to understand the underlying logic that governs the entire system — operating days, notifications, language, data handling, and what is intentionally excluded from v1.
E1
The 6 PM Cutoff
A system-wide rule: any change that would affect the next day's delivery must be made before 6 PM. After 6 PM, those actions are locked until the following day.
What the 6 PM Cutoff Applies To
Skip a Day
A delivery can only be skipped before 6 PM on the day before that delivery. After 6 PM, the skip button is hidden or disabled for the next day's meal. Example: to skip a Monday delivery, the customer must skip before Sunday 6 PM.
Undo a Skip
An already-set skip can be reversed before 6 PM on the day before delivery. After that cutoff, the skip is final.
Switch Meal Type
Switching between Executive and Salad for the next delivery day must be done before 6 PM. A switch made after 6 PM applies from the day after next.
Admin: Production stage Locked
The Locked stage in the Daily Ops dashboard signals that the 6 PM cutoff has passed. Skip counts are now final, the meal list is frozen, and label printing can begin with accurate counts.
Operating Week & Timezone
Timezone
All cutoff times are in Riyadh time — Arabia Standard Time (AST), UTC+3. There is no daylight saving time adjustment in Saudi Arabia.
Working week
Sunday to Thursday. This is the Saudi working week. Friday and Saturday are weekend days with no deliveries.
Weekend cutoff example
For a Sunday delivery (the first working day of the week), the skip/change cutoff is Saturday at 6 PM. This is the most important example — it means customers must manage their Sunday delivery on Saturday evening, not Sunday morning.
How the UI Enforces the Cutoff

After 6 PM passes for a given delivery day, the following happen automatically in the customer app: Skip buttons are disabled or hidden for that meal's card on the home screen and in the Skip a Day list. The Meal Detail bottom sheet shows the skip button as inactive with a reason shown. Any active skip for that day can no longer be undone. The customer sees a clear indication of why the action is unavailable — the cutoff time is communicated transparently.

E2
Notifications — All Push
Every automated notification in v1 is delivered as a push notification to the customer's device. WhatsApp and SMS are used exclusively for OTP verification at sign-in — for nothing else.
The Rule
🔔
Push notifications only — WhatsApp and SMS are for OTP only
This is a deliberate v1 decision. All automated messages are push notifications. WhatsApp and SMS are used at sign-in to deliver the 4-digit OTP code — either channel is available for that step. After sign-in, the app communicates exclusively via push notifications. There are no WhatsApp messages, no SMS messages, and no email notifications in v1.
Complete Push Notification List
Delivery Confirmed
Trigger: Driver taps "Mark as Delivered" for that customer's order.
Recipient: The customer whose meal was just delivered.
Content: Confirms the meal has arrived at their desk.
End-of-Day Feedback
Trigger: 3:00 PM daily.
Recipient: Every customer who received a meal that day.
Content: "How was today's meal?" with the meal name. Action buttons: "Rate now →" and "Later."
Renewal Reminder
Trigger: 48 hours before a customer's plan end date.
Recipient: The customer whose plan is ending.
Content: Plan name, end date, and a prompt to renew.
Referral Reward
Trigger: A referred friend's plan activates.
Recipient: The customer who shared their referral code.
Content: Confirmation of wallet credit earned (amount shown), new wallet balance.
Lapsed Reactivation
Trigger: Customer's plan has expired and no renewal made within 3 days.
Recipient: The lapsed customer.
Content: Invitation to come back and renew. (Automation toggle is Off by default — admin must enable.)
Issue Credit Notification
Trigger: Admin taps "Confirm Credit" in the Credit Wallet modal.
Recipient: The customer whose issue was credited.
Content: Credit amount applied and new wallet balance.
Broadcast Message
Trigger: Admin taps "Send" in the Broadcast tab of Comms & Automations.
Recipient: All customers in the selected segment (All active / Month plan / Lapsed / Delivering Today / Custom).
Content: Admin-composed free text message.
OTP — The Only WhatsApp / SMS Usage

When a customer or admin team member signs in, they enter their phone number and choose to receive a 4-digit OTP via WhatsApp or SMS. Both options are available and shown equally on the sign-in screen. The OTP is delivered through the chosen channel. This is the only point in the application where WhatsApp or SMS is used.

E3
Language (EN / AR)
The customer app supports both English and Arabic. A toggle on every screen allows the customer to switch between the two. The admin portal is English only.
Customer App Language
Toggle location
A compact EN / AR toggle appears in the top-right area of every customer-facing screen. Tapping switches the entire app UI to the selected language immediately — no reload required.
Arabic meal names
Every dish in the Meal Library has both an English name and an Arabic name. The Arabic name is entered by the admin when adding the dish. It appears below the English name on the Meal Detail screen and in the menu list rows regardless of the current language setting — both are always shown.
Arabic UI
When Arabic is selected, the app layout mirrors to right-to-left (RTL). Text, icons, and navigation elements follow standard RTL conventions. Fonts: Plus Jakarta Sans for English UI, Tajawal for Arabic UI.
Default language
English (EN) is the default. The customer's language preference is saved to their account and persists across sessions.
Admin Portal Language

The admin portal (all screens for Administrator, Ops/Kitchen, and Driver roles) is English only. There is no Arabic toggle in the admin portal in v1.

E4
Data Rules
Two specific rules about how customer-entered data is handled — both relating to free text input that is accepted but intentionally not persisted as structured data.
Customer Building Free Text
🏢
Customer free text building input is accepted but never stored in the building list
During onboarding or when editing their delivery address, a customer may type a building name that is not in the admin-managed auto-suggest list. This free text is accepted and saved to that customer's delivery record — their delivery will use the address they typed. However, it is never stored in the building database and will not appear as an auto-suggest option for any other customer. Only buildings added by the admin through the Delivery Areas & Buildings screen appear in the building auto-suggest.
Out-of-Zone Area Input
📍
Out-of-zone area names are captured for expansion planning only
When a customer types an area that is not in the admin's active area list during onboarding, they see a message: "We haven't expanded there yet." A free text field allows them to enter their area name. This text is captured to the backend as an expansion planning signal — it helps the business understand where unmet demand exists. It is not surfaced anywhere in the application (not shown in the admin portal, not shown to other customers, not stored as an area in the delivery system). The customer cannot proceed to plan selection from an out-of-zone area.
E5
What is Not in v1
A definitive list of features that do not exist in the current version of the application. These items may appear in earlier planning documents — this section is the authoritative record of what is excluded from v1.
Excluded Features
Salad Add-On
A planned feature that would allow customers to add a daily salad bowl to their existing Executive Meal subscription for +SAR 15 per delivery. Not in v1. No button, screen, pricing, or logic for this exists in the current application. The Salad Meal subscription type (a different thing) is fully supported.
Points / Loyalty system
A loyalty programme that would award points per meal or per referral. Not in v1. The Profile stats row shows Meals and Referrals only — there is no Points stat.
Photo upload on issue reports
Customers cannot attach a photo to their issue report in v1. The issue form has a description text field only. Photo evidence for complaints is a planned enhancement.
WhatsApp for non-OTP communication
WhatsApp is used only for OTP delivery at sign-in. No automated notifications, delivery confirmations, reminders, or broadcast messages are sent via WhatsApp in v1. All such communications are push notifications.
Meal History — Monthly View
A detailed month-grouped view of meal history was in earlier plans. It is present in the prototype file as a patch (Section 08b) but is not a supported v1 feature. The production build should not include it unless explicitly added in a future iteration.
Upgrade / downgrade plan framing
The application does not frame plan selection as upgrading or downgrading. All plan tiers are presented neutrally. There is no "Upgrade" button or screen in v1.
Salad Attach Rate KPI
This metric would track the percentage of customers adding the Salad Add-On to their order. Since the Salad Add-On is not in v1, this KPI does not appear on the Revenue Dashboard.
QR code delivery confirmation
An earlier version of the rider app planned QR code scanning to confirm delivery. Removed entirely. Delivery confirmation is done by tapping the "Mark as Delivered" button only.
E6
Known Build Corrections
Gaps between the current built prototype and the intended product specification. These corrections must be applied in the production build. Each item describes what is currently built, what the intended behaviour is, and what needs to change.
Correction 1 — Menu Not Accessible in Expired State
🔧
Current build: Menu tab hidden when plan is Expired
What is built: In the current prototype, the Menu tab in the bottom navigation is not visible or accessible when a customer's subscription is in the Expired state.

What is intended: The Menu screen must be accessible in all subscription states — Unsubscribed, Active, Expired, Paused, and Resumed. No meal should be locked or hidden at the menu level. A customer with an expired plan should be able to browse this week's menu freely, which supports re-engagement and renewal.

Fix required: Ensure the Menu tab is always present in the bottom navigation regardless of subscription state. The menu content itself does not change — only the skip action is unavailable for expired/unsubscribed users.
Correction 2 — Working Days Shown as Monday–Friday
🔧
Current build: Delivery days shown as Mon–Fri throughout prototype
What is built: The prototype shows Monday through Friday as the working week in meal card labels, calendar pickers, day pill selectors (Switch Meal Type), and date references throughout the customer app.

What is intended: The Saudi working week is Sunday to Thursday. All references to working days, delivery days, meal card dates, calendar grids, and day pills must reflect Sunday–Thursday.

Fix required: Update all day labels, calendar grids, and date logic throughout the application. The 6 PM cutoff logic must also account for Saturday being the pre-weekend cutoff day (for Sunday deliveries), not Sunday.
Correction 3 — Delivery Confirmation Shown as WhatsApp
🔧
Current build: Some screens reference WhatsApp for delivery confirmation
What is built: In some areas of the built prototype (including the Unsubscribed Home screen "Why Zaadi" value proposition section and some success screen copy), WhatsApp is referenced as the delivery confirmation channel.

What is intended: All delivery confirmations are push notifications. WhatsApp is used exclusively for OTP delivery at sign-in. No other communication happens via WhatsApp or SMS in v1.
