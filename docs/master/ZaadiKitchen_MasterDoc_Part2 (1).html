B
Customer Application — B10 to B17
Continues from Part 1 (B1–B9). Covers the remaining customer-facing features: plan cancellation, meal type switching, profile, referral, billing, feedback, meal history, and support.
B10
Cancel Plan
Customers can cancel their subscription at any time. Cancellation does not stop deliveries immediately — the plan runs until the paid end date with no refund. The process is designed to be clear about consequences before confirmation.
How Cancellation Works
Entry point
My Plan tab → Cancel Plan (shown in red at the bottom of the manage items list). Tapping this opens the Cancel Plan confirmation modal. The subscription is not cancelled immediately — confirmation is required.
Confirmation modal
A bottom sheet modal with a red title. Clearly states: (1) Deliveries continue until the paid end date, (2) No refund for remaining days, (3) Plan will not renew after the end date. A Contact Support link is also shown for customers who want to discuss alternatives.
After confirming
The cancellation flag is set in the backend. Deliveries continue normally until the last day of the paid period. On the end date, the subscription expires and the customer's home screen transitions to the Expired state.
No refunds
There are no refunds on cancelled plans. The customer has paid for a set number of meals and those meals continue to be delivered until the end date regardless of cancellation.
Contact Support
The modal includes a "Contact Support →" link for customers who want to discuss their situation before cancelling. This routes to the Support screen.
What the Modal Shows
❌
Exact content of the cancellation confirmation
The modal presents three explicit consequences before the customer can confirm:

✓ Deliveries continue until [end date] — the actual date is shown dynamically
✗ No refund for remaining days
✗ Plan will not renew after end date

Primary button: "Yes, cancel my plan" in red. Secondary button: "Keep my plan" (ghost). The two-button layout ensures the customer must make an active choice — there is no accidental cancellation.
B11
Switch Meal Type
Customers can switch between Executive Meal and Salad Meal at any time during their subscription. The switch can apply to all remaining days or to specific days of the week.
How It Works
Entry points
Two routes: (1) My Plan tab → Switch Meal Type. (2) The "Switch Meal Type" quick action tile on the Active home screen.
Selection
Two selectable cards: Executive Meal (🍛) and Salad Meal (🥗). Each shows the meal type name and kcal range. The currently active type is shown. The customer taps to select a new type — the card highlights with a mint border and checkmark.
Scope — entire subscription
The default option. Applies the new meal type to all remaining delivery days in the current subscription period.
Scope — specific days
The customer can choose to switch only on selected days. Tapping "Specific days" reveals day pills (Sun / Mon / Tue / Wed / Thu — reflecting the Saudi working week). Pills are multi-select — the customer can choose any combination of days.
6 PM cutoff
Changes to meal type for the next delivery day must be made before 6 PM. Changes are effective from the following day's delivery.
Confirm Switch
Tapping the Confirm Switch button applies the change and returns to the My Plan tab. The meal type shown on upcoming home screen meal cards updates to reflect the new selection.
Change Plan

Switch Meal Type is different from Change Plan. Change Plan (also on the My Plan tab) routes the customer to the Plan Selection screen where they can choose a different subscription tier for their next period — Try It, Week Plan, Month Plan, or Quarterly Plan.

✅
No upgrade or downgrade framing
The Change Plan screen presents all four plan tiers neutrally. There is no language suggesting a customer is "upgrading" or "downgrading." All plan choices are equally valid and the customer selects whichever suits them.
B12
Profile
The customer's account hub. Shows two summary stats and links to all account management screens. Accessible from the bottom navigation Profile tab.
Profile Header
Avatar
Circle showing the customer's name initial in a mint background. No photo upload in v1.
Name and phone
Full name and phone number shown beneath the avatar.
EN / AR toggle
Language toggle in the top-right of the header. Switches the entire app between English and Arabic.
Stats Row
📊
Two stats only — Meals and Referrals
The profile shows exactly two lifetime stats: Meals (total meals delivered across all subscriptions) and Referrals (number of friends who joined using this customer's referral code). A points or loyalty stat is not in v1 and does not appear anywhere in the profile.
Profile Sections
MY SUBSCRIPTION
Three items: Manage Plan (shows days remaining in sub-label) · Skip Days · Switch Meal Type. These are shortcuts to subscription management without going through the My Plan tab.
ACCOUNT
Six items: Rate Your Meals (shows a badge with count of unrated meals) · Meal History · Billing & Wallet · Refer Friends · Delivery Address · Edit Profile.
Rate Your Meals Badge

The "Rate Your Meals" item in the Account section shows a badge with the number of meals that have been delivered but not yet rated. For example, if the customer received 3 meals since their last rating session, the badge shows "3." The badge decrements by 1 each time a meal is rated. It disappears when all pending ratings are submitted.

B13
Referral
Every customer has a permanent unique referral code. Sharing it gives a new user 20% off their first plan. The referrer earns 10% of that purchase as wallet credit the moment the friend's plan goes live.
How the Referral Programme Works
Referral code
Each customer has one permanent unique code (e.g. AHMED15). It never changes and there is no limit to how many friends they can refer with it.
New user benefit
20% off their first subscription plan. Applies to all four plan types (Try It, Week, Month, Quarterly). The code is entered on the payment page at checkout. The order total updates immediately with the discount shown as a line item.
Referral code validity
Valid on the new user's first subscription only. The same code cannot be reused by the same customer on any subsequent renewal.
Referrer reward
10% of the new user's first plan purchase as wallet credit. Credited immediately when the new user's plan activates — not when they pay, but when their plan goes live and the first delivery period begins.
Reward amount
Variable — based on 10% of whichever plan the new user purchased. Examples: Month Plan (SAR 500) = SAR 50 credit. Week Plan (SAR 125) = SAR 12.50 credit. Quarterly Plan (SAR 1,300) = SAR 130 credit.
Referral history
The Referral screen shows a list of referred friends with: name (or initial), when they joined, which plan they chose, and the exact SAR credit earned. Amounts are shown as variable figures (not a fixed SAR 28 or any other fixed amount).
Sharing the Code

The referral code is displayed in a prominent dashed lemon-coloured box on the Referral screen. Long-pressing the code copies it to the clipboard. A "Share via WhatsApp" button opens the WhatsApp share sheet with a pre-composed message containing the code and an app link.

The Referral screen also shows two aggregate stats: total friends who have joined using the code, and total SAR earned in referral wallet credits across all referrals.

B14
Billing & Wallet
The Billing & Wallet screen shows the customer's wallet balance, their saved payment cards, active promo codes, and a full transaction history. Accessible from both the Home quick actions row and the Profile screen.
Screen Sections
Wallet balance
Shown prominently in the green gradient header at the top of the screen. Displays the current available balance in SAR. This balance is automatically applied at the next checkout.
Saved card chips
A row of chips showing saved payment methods (e.g. Mada, Visa ····4242, Apple Pay). Tapping a chip sets it as the default for future payments.
Promo code cards
Active promo codes shown as cards with a dashed border and an "Active" badge. Tapping a promo card shows when it expires and which plan types it applies to.
Transaction history
A chronological list of all financial activity on the account. Each entry shows: date, description, and amount (credits in green, charges in orange). Tapping an entry opens the full receipt detail for that transaction.
Transaction Entry Types
Credits (+SAR)
Money added to wallet
Referral rewards — shown as "10% of SAR [plan price]" so the source is clear. Issue credits — shown as "Wallet credit — [issue type]."
Charges (−SAR)
Payments made
Plan purchases — shown as "[Plan name] · [Month/Year]." Wallet deductions applied at checkout are shown as a separate line item within the plan charge entry.
B15
Feedback & Ratings
Customers are invited to rate each meal after delivery. Ratings are collected via a push notification at 3 PM and through the Rate Your Meals screen. Feedback goes to the kitchen team to inform daily production.
End-of-Day Feedback Push Notification
Trigger
3 PM daily, sent as a push notification to every customer who received a meal that day.
Content
"⭐ How was today's meal?" with the meal name shown. Two action buttons: "Rate now →" (opens the feedback screen directly) and "Later" (dismisses the notification, rating stays pending).
Communication channel
Push notification only. This is not sent via WhatsApp or SMS. WhatsApp and SMS are used for OTP at sign-in only.
If dismissed
The rating remains as a pending item. The "Rate Your Meals" item in the Profile screen shows a badge with the pending count. The customer can rate at any time from there.
Feedback Form
Star rating
5 stars. Tapping any star fills 1 through that value. "Poor" label at the 1-star end, "Excellent" at 5 stars. Minimum 1 star required to submit. The customer can change their rating before submitting.
Feedback tags
Multi-select chip tags — optional. Options: Great portion · Too spicy · Too salty · Small portion. Multiple can be selected simultaneously. Submitted with the star rating.
Text comment
Optional free text field. Any length. Submitted with the rating.
Submit
Requires minimum 1 star. Once submitted, the rating cannot be changed. Stored against the meal record in the admin dashboard for the kitchen team to review. The Profile badge decrements by 1.
Rate Last 5 Meals Screen

Accessed from Profile → Rate Your Meals or by tapping "Rate now →" in the push notification. Shows up to 5 recent delivered meals.

Pending meals appear at the top with a mint border. Each pending card shows: meal thumbnail, name, kcal, delivery date, the star rating input, feedback tag chips, an optional comment field, and a Submit button.

Already-rated meals appear below pending ones. Each shows: the star rating that was given, and a green "✅ Feedback submitted · Thank you!" confirmation banner. Submitted ratings cannot be changed.

Where Feedback Goes

All submitted ratings and comments are stored against the meal record in the admin dashboard. The kitchen team uses this data for production insights — identifying which dishes are consistently rated lower, which feedback tags are most common, and tracking quality trends over time. This data also informs the Menu Manager when the admin is deciding which dishes to mark as starred favourites.

B16
Meal History
A calorie tracker and meal log showing the customer's delivery history. Visualises intake trends with a bar chart and stats. Accessible from Profile → Meal History.
Dark Header Summary
Calorie total
Total kcal consumed this month based on delivered (non-skipped) meals. Shown in a large Fraunces typeface.
Goal badge
Shows the percentage of the customer's monthly calorie goal achieved (e.g. "68% of goal"). Goal is set or estimated based on meal type and frequency.
5-day bar chart
Visualises the last 5 delivery days. Bar colours: Mint = delivered meal · Papaya = high-calorie day · Grey/faded = skipped day (0 kcal). Tapping a bar shows the exact kcal count and date in a tooltip.
Mini stats row
Three figures: This Week total kcal · This Month total kcal · Average kcal per meal.
Tabs and List View
Last 5 Days tab
Default view. Shows the 5 most recent delivery days grouped by date. Each day lists the meal(s) delivered on that day.
This Month tab
Shows all delivery days in the current calendar month, week-grouped.
All (30) tab
Shows the last 30 delivered meals across all subscription periods.
Delivered rows
Each row shows: meal thumbnail · meal name · meal type · delivery time · kcal count · star rating (if rated). Tapping opens a read-only Meal Detail view — no skip action on past meals.
Skipped rows
Shown at 50% opacity with a ⏭️ icon. Meal name shown but greyed. Kcal shows a dash (—). No tap action.
B17
Support
The support screen provides two pathways: reporting a delivery issue and browsing the FAQ. Accessible from the bottom navigation Support tab.
Support Hub — Two Tiles Only
🚨 Report an Issue
For delivery problems
Wrong order, quality issues, not delivered, late delivery. Opens the issue report form.
❓ FAQ
Common questions answered
How skipping works, the skip limit, pausing, switching meal types, and other frequently asked questions.
ℹ️
Only two tiles in v1
The Support screen shows exactly two tiles: Report an Issue and FAQ. There is no "Chat with Us" tile, no "Suggest a Meal" tile, and no live chat function in v1.
Report an Issue — Form
Issue type chips
Four single-select chips: Wrong order · Quality issue · Not delivered · Late delivery. One must be selected before the form can be submitted.
Today's meal
Pre-filled automatically with today's delivered meal (name, type, and delivery time). Read-only — provides context to the ops team reviewing the issue without the customer needing to describe it.
Description
Optional free text area for the customer to describe what happened. No photo upload in v1.
Submit
Requires an issue type chip to be selected. Submission creates an issue record in the admin ops queue.
What Happens After Submission
Customer submits
→
Issue record created in admin queue
→
Ops team reviews within 2 hours
→
Customer notified of outcome
Outcome notification is sent as a push notification. If the admin credits the customer's wallet as part of the resolution, a separate push notification confirms the credit amount and new wallet balance.
FAQ Quick Links

Below the two tiles, the Support Hub shows four FAQ quick links covering the most commonly asked questions: When is the skip cutoff? · How does the skip limit work? · How do I pause my plan? · Can I switch meal types? Tapping any link opens that specific FAQ article directly.

C
Admin Portal — C1 to C5
The first five sections of the Admin Portal: roles and access, the admin dashboard home, daily operations, issue resolution, and label printing. Sections C6–C10 continue in Part 3.
C1
Roles & Access
The admin portal has three distinct roles. Each role authenticates through the same login screen but lands on a different dashboard with a different set of permissions.
The Three Roles
Administrator
Full access to all admin sections: Daily Ops (with full issue resolution), Revenue Dashboard, Menu Manager, Customer Management, Comms & Automations, and Delivery Areas & Buildings. Lands on the Admin Dashboard Home tile screen after login.
Ops / Kitchen
Daily Ops and Print Labels only. Can escalate issues but cannot credit wallets or reject issues. Cannot access Revenue, Menu Manager, Customer Management, Comms, or Delivery Areas. Lands directly on the Daily Ops screen after login — there is no tile dashboard for this role.

Role card description (exact wording): "Daily ops, production tracking, label printing. No access to revenue, menu, or customer data."
Driver
Rider App only. Sees only their personal delivery list for the day. Cannot access any admin screen, customer data, or financial information.

Login Flow
Login screen
All three roles use the same login screen. Three role cards are shown — Administrator, Ops/Kitchen, and Driver. The team member taps their role card (which highlights it with a selection indicator) then enters their phone number. The sign-in button label updates to reflect the selected role: "Sign in as Ops / Kitchen →".
Authentication
Phone number OTP — 4-digit code. Same method as the customer app. Sent via WhatsApp or SMS to the registered phone number.
Post-login routing
Administrator → Admin Dashboard Home (tile grid). Ops/Kitchen → Daily Ops screen (limited view). Driver → Rider App (My Deliveries).
C2
Admin Dashboard Home
The landing screen for the Administrator role. A tile grid linking to all six admin sections. The Ops/Kitchen role does not see this screen — they land directly on Daily Ops.
The Six Tiles
1. Daily Ops
Today's meal count, production status, and a live red badge showing the number of open customer issues. This tile has an orange top accent to signal it is the primary daily action. Tapping opens the full Admin Daily Ops screen.
2. Revenue
Shows the current MRR figure. Tapping opens the Revenue Dashboard.
3. Menu Manager
Shows which week is currently being managed and how many of the 5 days have meals assigned (e.g. "Week of Apr 7 · 3/5 filled"). Tapping opens the Menu Manager.
4. Customers
Shows the current total of active subscribers. Tapping opens the Customer Management screen.
5. Comms
Shows how many automations are currently active (e.g. "5 automations active"). Tapping opens Comms & Automations.
6. Areas & Buildings
Shows a count of active areas and coming-soon areas (e.g. "3 active · 2 coming soon"). Tapping opens the Delivery Areas & Buildings screen.
Live Issue Badge
🔴
The Daily Ops tile shows a live count of open issues
The Daily Ops tile includes a red badge that shows the number of customer issues currently open and unresolved. This updates in real time as issues are submitted, resolved, or escalated. A count of "0" means the badge is not shown. This is the only live data element on the Dashboard Home screen.
C3
Daily Ops Dashboard
The primary operational screen used every working day. Shows today's production status, meal breakdown, and open customer issues. Available to both Administrator (full) and Ops/Kitchen (limited) roles with different issue resolution options.
Production Stages
📋
Three stages only — Locked → Dispatch → Delivered
The daily production flow has exactly three stages. Cooking and Packing stages do not exist in v1. The three stages are advanced manually by the admin or ops team by tapping the active stage indicator.
Locked
The 6 PM cutoff has passed. Skip requests are finalised. The meal count for the day is frozen. Label printing can proceed. This is the start-of-day state each morning — the ops team can see exactly how many meals to prepare and for whom.
Dispatch
Meals have been packed, labelled, and handed to the rider(s). The rider app is now active and deliveries are in progress. This is the active delivery state.
Delivered
All deliveries for the day are complete. The counter shows how many were delivered successfully versus flagged with issues. End-of-day feedback push notifications go out at 3 PM regardless of whether this stage has been reached.
Summary Stats (Top of Screen)
Total Meals
Today's meal count
Total active subscriptions receiving a delivery today. Frozen after the Locked stage.
Open Issues
Unresolved reports
Customer issue submissions that have not yet been actioned. Shown in orange to signal urgency.
Skipped
Today's skip count
Customers who skipped today's delivery before the 6 PM cutoff. Not included in the meal count.
Meal Breakdown Table

Below the stats, a simple table shows the meal count split by type:

Executive Meal row
Total count of Executive Meal subscriptions receiving a delivery today.
Salad Meal row
Total count of Salad Meal subscriptions receiving a delivery today.
Total row
Sum of both rows — matches the Total Meals stat at the top of the screen.
No add-on column
The breakdown table has no salad add-on column. The Salad Add-On feature is not in v1.
Export Sheet

An "Export" button on the Daily Ops screen generates a downloadable spreadsheet containing: customer name, delivery address (building, floor, desk, gate), meal type, and any rider notes — one row per delivery. This is used by the ops team for production planning and rider routing. The export reflects the final Locked state count and is most accurate after the 6 PM cutoff has passed.

Role Differences on This Screen
Ops/Kitchen role
Sees the same stats, stage indicator, meal breakdown, and issue list. On each issue row, only an Escalate button is shown. A note states "Credit wallet: Admin only" to make the limitation clear. Cannot credit wallets or reject issues.
Administrator role
Sees the same screen but each issue row shows three actions: Credit Wallet · Escalate · Reject. Full issue resolution capability.
C4
Issue Resolution
Customer issues submitted via the Support screen appear in the admin Daily Ops dashboard as open issue rows. The Administrator has three resolution actions. The Ops/Kitchen role can only escalate.
Issue Row Structure

Each open issue appears as a card with a left orange border. The card shows: issue type (e.g. "Wrong order"), the customer's name and delivery address, and the customer's description text in italics. Below the description are the action buttons available for that role.

The Three Resolution Actions (Administrator Only)
💳 Credit Wallet
Opens the Credit Wallet modal. Admin enters a custom SAR amount. Maximum cap is SAR 30 per issue. Quick preset buttons (SAR 10, SAR 20, SAR 28, SAR 30) speed up common amounts. If the entered amount exceeds SAR 30, an error is shown inline and the confirm button is disabled. On confirmation: the credit is applied to the customer's wallet immediately, and a push notification is sent to the customer confirming the credit amount and new balance.
↑ Escalate
Flags the issue for senior management review. Moves the issue to an "Escalated" queue. Available to both Administrator and Ops/Kitchen roles. Use this when the issue requires a decision or action beyond the standard resolution options.
✕ Reject
Closes the issue without any credit or action. Requires a reason to be selected from a dropdown before confirming. The three reason options are: (1) Not a valid issue, (2) Duplicate report, (3) Outside policy. No credit is issued. The issue is marked as closed.
Credit Wallet — Maximum Cap Detail
💳
SAR 30 maximum per issue — enforced at the field level
The Credit Wallet modal enforces a maximum of SAR 30 per issue resolution. This cap is applied in the input field itself — if the admin enters an amount above SAR 30, the field highlights in red, an error message appears, and the Confirm button becomes inactive until the amount is corrected. The cap cannot be bypassed. Quick preset buttons make common amounts one tap: SAR 10 · SAR 20 · SAR 28 · SAR 30.

C5
Print Labels
The label printing screen generates thermal sticker labels for every delivery that day. Accessible from the Daily Ops screen via the Print Labels button. Available to both Administrator and Ops/Kitchen roles.
What the Label Contains
Label Content
All fields from the customer's delivery address
Zaadi Kitchen logo · Customer full name · Building name · Floor · Desk / Area · Gate / Entrance · Meal type (colour-coded: orange band for Executive, green band for Salad) · Delivery date · Order reference number.
Label Format
100×60mm thermal sticker
Designed for standard thermal label printers. Downloaded as a PDF. Each label is pre-formatted — no additional layout work required before printing.
Download Options
Bulk download — all
A single PDF containing all labels for today, sorted by area. One tap at the top of the screen. Fastest option for printing a full day's labels.
Individual download
Each customer row has a Download button and a Preview button. Preview opens a full-size visual of that specific label before downloading.
Area-level download
At the bottom of the label list, buttons allow downloading all labels for a single delivery area as one PDF — e.g. "Al Nakheel (32)" downloads only the 32 labels for Al Nakheel. Useful when different riders handle different areas.
Filter by Meal Type

Three filter chips at the top of the label list: All · Executive (Exec) · Salad. Selecting a chip filters the list to show only labels for that meal type. The bulk download button updates to reflect the filtered count — e.g. "Download Exec labels (63)." This is useful when Executive and Salad meals are packed separately on the kitchen line.

When to Print
⏰
Print after the Locked stage — not before
Labels should be printed after the Daily Ops production stage moves to Locked (post-6 PM cutoff the previous evening). At this point the skip count is finalised and the meal list will not change. Printing before the Locked stage risks producing labels for meals that may subsequently be skipped, resulting in wasted labels.
