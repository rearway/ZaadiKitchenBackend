A
Business Foundation
What Zaadi Kitchen is, how it operates, and the rules that govern the business. Read this section first — it provides the context behind every product and design decision covered in Parts B, C, and D.
A1
What is Zaadi Kitchen
A neighbourhood kitchen that cooks fresh meals every morning and delivers them to office professionals at their desks in Riyadh, Sunday to Thursday.
The Concept

Zaadi Kitchen solves a specific daily problem: office workers in Riyadh need a reliable, fresh, high-quality lunch without the friction of ordering, waiting, or leaving their desk. The kitchen prepares all meals fresh on the morning of delivery — nothing is frozen, reheated, or prepared the previous day. Meals are delivered during the lunch window directly to the customer's desk.

The business runs on a subscription model. Customers pay upfront for a block of meals and receive deliveries on working days within their plan period. There is no auto-renewal — customers must actively choose to continue.

Core Promise to Customers
Freshness
Cooked that same morning
No frozen food. No reheating. Every meal is prepared on the day of delivery.
Convenience
Delivered to your desk
Customer sets building, floor, and desk. Rider delivers to that exact location. Push notification sent on arrival.
Flexibility
Skip any day, no hassle
No daily commitment. Skip before 6 PM with no penalty. Pause for travel or holidays. Cancel with no friction.
Trust
Halal and preservative-free
Stated to all customers at sign-up. No allergen collection required in v1 — this is a baseline guarantee.
Operating Scope — v1
Geography
Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. Delivery is limited to specific areas managed by admin. Customers can only sign up if their area is active.
Delivery days
Sunday to Thursday only. This is the Saudi working week. No Friday or Saturday deliveries. Public holidays are excluded.
Delivery window
Lunch time. The exact time is communicated via push notification the day of delivery. Evening-before preview is planned for future versions.
Meal types
Two types available each day: Executive Meal and Salad Meal. Customer selects their default type at subscription and can switch at any time.
Not in v1
Salad add-on (daily extra item at +SAR 15). Loyalty and points system. Weekend or public holiday delivery. Multiple concurrent subscriptions per account.

How Plans Work
Payment
Full plan price paid upfront at checkout. No instalments or partial payments.
Start date
Customer selects on the payment page using a calendar picker. Default is the next working day (Sunday–Thursday). Can be changed to any future working day.
Auto-renewal
There is no auto-renewal on any plan. When the plan period ends, deliveries stop. The customer must actively return to the app and choose a new plan. A renewal nudge appears in the app when 20% or fewer of the plan's days remain.
Working days
Sunday to Thursday only. Weekends (Friday and Saturday) and public holidays are not delivery days. The plan period spans calendar days, but deliveries happen only on working days within that span.
Plan switching
When renewing, the customer can choose any plan tier — the app does not frame any choice as an "upgrade" or "downgrade." All plans are presented equally on the Plan Selection screen.
Refunds
No refunds on any plan. If a customer cancels, deliveries continue until the paid end date. The plan does not renew after that point.
The 2× Skip and Pause Window
📌
Core rule governing all skips and pauses
Every plan has a time window within which the customer must use all their skips and pauses. The window is 2× the plan duration, measured from the plan start date. Week Plan = 3 weeks. Month Plan = 3 months. Quarterly Plan = 9 months. Once this window closes, skipping and pausing are no longer available for that subscription period. The window resets completely when the customer renews — it does not carry over.
A3
Meal Types
Two meal types are offered every working day. Customers choose their default at subscription and can switch at any time through the app.
The Two Types
Executive Meal 🍛
~500–600 kcal
Arabic rice dish with a protein (chicken, lamb, or fish) and sides. Hearty and filling. Examples: Lamb Kabsa, Grilled Chicken Mandi, Chicken Haneeth.
Salad Meal 🥗
~300–350 kcal
Grilled protein on fresh greens with dressing. Lighter, lower-carb option. Examples: Chicken Fattoush, Grilled Salmon Bowl, Tabbouleh & Kofta Bowl.
Critical Distinction — Salad Meal vs Salad Add-On
⚠️
These are two entirely different things
Salad Meal is a subscription meal type — the customer's daily meal is a salad bowl instead of a rice dish. This is fully supported in v1 and available on all plans.

Salad Add-On was a planned feature allowing customers to add a daily salad on top of their Executive Meal for +SAR 15. This is not in v1 and does not appear anywhere in the application. No button, no screen, no charge for it exists in the current build.
Menu Cadence
Who sets the menu
The Administrator, using the Menu Manager in the admin portal. A new menu is planned and published each week.
Frequency
Weekly. One Executive Meal and one Salad Meal are assigned per working day (Sunday–Thursday). The admin must fill all 5 days before the Publish button becomes active.
When customers see it
Once the admin publishes the week's menu, it appears immediately in the customer's Menu screen (7-day view). The menu is visible to all users regardless of their subscription status.
Macros displayed
Calories, protein, carbs, and fat — shown per meal on the Meal Detail screen. Values are set by the admin when adding a dish to the Meal Library.
Chef's Note
Each dish can include a short description in the chef's voice. Shown on the Meal Detail screen in a lemon-coloured box with italic text.

Delivery Confirmation — Push Notification
🔔
All customer notifications are push notifications
Delivery confirmation is sent as a push notification to the customer's device when the rider marks their order as delivered. WhatsApp and SMS are used exclusively for OTP verification at sign-in — they are not used for any other communication in v1.
Address Structure

The delivery address is built in layers during onboarding and can be updated at any time.

Area
The delivery zone — e.g. "Al Nakheel", "Olaya Business District." Admin-managed list. Customers select from a dropdown. Out-of-zone areas cannot be selected — the customer's interest is captured for expansion planning but no delivery is possible.
Building
The office building — e.g. "Al Nakheel Tower, King Fahad Rd." Auto-suggest list populated by admin. Customers can type a building not in the list, but that entry is never stored and will not appear as a suggestion for anyone else.
Floor
Optional. Shown on the delivery label and the rider's delivery list. Example: "Floor 4".
Desk / Area
Optional. The specific desk or area within the floor. Example: "Desk B12", "Marketing section".
Gate / Entrance
Optional. Which building entrance the rider should use. Example: "Main lobby", "Gate B".
Rider Notes
Optional free text. Shown on the rider's delivery row for this customer. Example: "Call me on arrival", "Leave at reception if I'm in a meeting".
Delivery preference
Hand to me directly — or — Leave at reception. Set by the customer. Shown on the rider's delivery row so they know what to do on arrival.
Sticker Labels

Every meal package carries a 100×60mm thermal sticker label. The label contains: customer name, building, floor, desk, gate, meal type, delivery date, and order reference number. Labels are generated from the admin portal by the ops team and printed before dispatch. Downloads are available as: all labels in one PDF, individual per customer, or grouped by delivery area.

Multiple Saved Addresses

Customers can save more than one address — for example, a primary office and a secondary location. One address is marked as Active at any time and receives all deliveries. Switching the active address takes effect from the next scheduled delivery.

A5
Pricing, Wallet & Payments
How money flows through the application — from initial payment to wallet credits, promo codes, and the referral programme.
Payment Methods
Apple Pay
Default for new users
Pre-selected as the quickest option when no saved card exists on the account.
Mada
Pre-selected for returning users
The last saved card is pre-selected automatically at checkout for returning customers.
Visa / Mastercard
Also available
Credit or debit card option. Available to all users on every payment page.
STC Pay
Also available
Mobile wallet option. Available to all users on every payment page.
Wallet System
What the wallet is
An in-app credit balance. Shown as "SAR X.XX" in the Billing & Wallet screen. Applied automatically at checkout for returning users.
How credits are earned
Two sources: (1) Referral rewards — 10% of a referred friend's first plan price, credited immediately when their plan activates. (2) Issue credits — admin manually credits an amount (up to SAR 30 maximum) when resolving a valid customer issue. Credit triggers a push notification to the customer.
How wallet is applied
For returning users, the wallet balance is automatically deducted from the total due and shown as a line item in the order summary on the payment page. The customer pays the remaining balance. There is no option to opt out of wallet application.
Expiry
Wallet credit does not expire. It remains until used and is not tied to a plan cycle or subscription period.
Promo Codes & Referral Programme
📋
Where codes are entered
Promo and referral codes are entered on the payment page, above the payment method selection. The Plan Selection screen shows a teaser message ("Have a referral code? Enter it at checkout") but has no input field. The order total updates immediately when a valid code is applied.
Promo code
20% off the plan price. Applies to all four plan types. The discounted total is reflected immediately in the order summary.
Referral code — new user
Same 20% off. However, referral codes are only valid on a customer's first subscription. If a returning user attempts to apply a referral code, it is shown as invalid.
Referral reward — referrer
The person who shared their code earns 10% of the new user's first plan purchase as wallet credit. This credit is applied immediately when the new user's plan activates — not at the moment of payment, but when the plan goes live. The amount varies: 10% of SAR 500 = SAR 50 for a Month Plan referral.
Referral code validity
Each customer has a permanent unique referral code. There is no limit to how many friends they can refer. The 20% discount applies only to the new user's first plan — they cannot use a referral code on any subsequent renewal.
Refund Policy
❌
No refunds on any plan
Zaadi Kitchen does not offer refunds. If a customer cancels their plan, deliveries continue until the last day of the paid period. The plan will not renew after that date. Customers who wish to discuss their situation further are directed to the support team via the Contact Support link in the cancellation confirmation screen.
B
Customer Application — B1 to B9
Every feature a customer interacts with: what it does, the rules behind it, and how the application executes it. Sections B1–B9 are covered here. B10–B17 continue in Part 2 of this document.
B1
Sign In & Authentication
How customers get into the app. Two OTP options are shown equally on the same screen. A 4-digit code is used. The app routes to different screens depending on whether the user is new or returning.
Authentication Options
📱
WhatsApp and SMS are used for OTP only — nothing else
In v1, WhatsApp and SMS are the two methods for delivering the sign-in OTP code. They are not used for any other purpose. All other notifications — delivery confirmation, feedback requests, renewal reminders, referral rewards, and broadcast messages — are sent as push notifications, not via WhatsApp or SMS.
WhatsApp OTP
Primary option
4-digit code sent via WhatsApp to the customer's phone number. Most customers will have WhatsApp — this is the recommended path.
SMS OTP
Equal option
Same 4-digit code delivered via SMS. Shown on the same screen as WhatsApp — not a fallback. Both options are equally available.
ℹ️
4-digit code — important for developers
The OTP is exactly 4 digits. The UI shows 4 input boxes. The backend must generate and validate 4-digit codes only. This is different from the 6-digit standard used by many applications.
Post-Authentication Routing
Existing user
Routes directly to the Home screen in whatever state their subscription is in — Active, Expired, Paused, or Resumed.
New user
Routes to the Name Entry screen to complete their profile before proceeding to area selection and plan selection.
Name Entry — New Users Only

Name is required — the app cannot proceed without it. The name is used in all personalised greetings throughout the app ("Good morning, Ahmed 👋") and on the Plan Selection screen for returning users ("Welcome back, Ahmed!").

Email is optional and can be skipped entirely or added later from the Edit Profile screen in the customer's account. A green banner on the Name Entry screen confirms that all meals are Halal and preservative-free.

B2
Onboarding: Area & Building
After entering their name, new customers set their delivery location. This is a two-step process — area first, then building details. All data comes from admin-managed lists. Customer free text input is accepted but never stored in those lists.
Step 1 — Area Search
What the customer sees
A search bar with a dropdown list of delivery areas. Each area shows its distance from the kitchen and an in-zone confirmation badge.
Where the list comes from
Populated entirely from the admin portal (Delivery Areas section). Only areas with "Active" status are selectable. "Coming Soon" areas appear in the list but are greyed out and cannot be chosen.
Out-of-zone areas
If the customer types an area not in the list, a message is shown: "We haven't expanded there yet." The customer can enter their area as free text. This is captured in the backend for expansion planning only — it is never shown to other customers and does not create an autosuggest entry.
Cannot proceed out-of-zone
A customer in an out-of-zone area cannot continue to plan selection. They must use a different address within an active delivery area.
Step 2 — Building Details
Building field
Shows an auto-suggest list as the customer types. The list is populated by the admin from the Buildings section within each area. If a customer types a building not in the list, the free text is accepted — but it is never stored and will not appear as a suggestion for any other customer. Only the admin can add buildings to the official list.
Floor
Optional. Appears on the delivery sticker label and on the rider's delivery list for that customer.
Desk / Area
Optional. Helps the rider find the exact desk within the floor.
Gate / Entrance
Optional. Which building entrance to use.
Rider Notes
Optional free text. Shown to the rider on their delivery list for this customer. Example: "Call me on arrival", "Leave at reception if I'm in a meeting."
Minimum to proceed
The Building field is the only required field. All others (floor, desk, gate, notes) are optional. The Save & Continue button is available once the building field is filled.
Editing the Address Later

Customers can update their delivery location at any time from two entry points: the "Edit" link on the location strip on the Home screen, or via Profile → Delivery Address. Multiple addresses can be saved; one is marked as Active at any time. Changes take effect from the next scheduled delivery.

B3
Plan Selection
The screen where customers choose their subscription plan. The presentation differs slightly between new and returning users, but the available plans and the neutral tone are the same.
New User Plan Selection

New users see all 4 plans. The Month Plan is featured in a dark card with a "Most Popular" tag. A meal type toggle at the top (Executive / Salad) allows the customer to view plans in the context of their preferred type — this does not change prices. A teaser at the top of the screen reads "Have a referral code? Enter it at checkout" — there is no input field on this screen.

Returning User Plan Selection

Returning users see a "Welcome back, [Name]!" header and a "Returning subscriber" banner. Their most recent plan is tagged "Your Last Plan" — this is informational only. They are equally free to choose any of the 4 plans.

✅
No upgrade or downgrade language
Plan selection is intentionally neutral. The app does not frame any plan as an "upgrade" or "downgrade" relative to a customer's previous plan. All plans are presented on equal footing — the customer chooses whatever suits them for the next period.

B5
Home Screen
The central daily screen for all customers. It has five distinct states based on subscription status. Each state shows different content, different quick actions, and a different bottom navigation tab.
The Five States
Unsubscribed
For new or lapsed customers with no active plan. Dark hero with "Start for SAR 28 →" primary CTA and "See menu" secondary. Meal cards are visible without locks — they show today's meals with a Subscribe button. Why Zaadi value props listed below the cards. Bottom nav shows "Plans" tab (not "My Plan").
Active
Standard daily view. Dark subscription banner with plan name and days remaining. Location strip shows the delivery address. Horizontal meal card scroll for the week ahead. 1×3 quick actions: Switch Meal Type / My Plan / Billing & Wallet. Bottom nav shows "My Plan" tab.
Expired
Plan has ended. Dark banner with orange "Expired" pill. Orange alert card with a Renew CTA. Meal cards are locked. 1×3 quick actions: Renew / Meal History / Billing & Wallet.
Paused
Subscription is paused. Purple banner and purple card showing: days frozen, paused-on date, and resumes date. Resume Subscription → CTA in the purple card. 1×3 quick actions: Resume Now / Browse Menu / Billing & Wallet.
Resumed
Immediately after resuming from pause. "Resumed" green pill in the active subscription banner. "You're back!" greeting. Upcoming meal cards visible. 1×3 quick actions: same as Active state.
Location Strip

The location strip appears below the subscription banner in the Active and Resumed states. It shows the customer's current active delivery address: building, floor, and desk. Tapping "Edit" opens the Edit Location screen. The location strip is not shown in the Unsubscribed, Expired, or Paused states.

Meal Card Scroll

The horizontal card scroll shows the current week's meals (up to 5 cards — Sunday through Thursday). Today's card is highlighted with a "TODAY" badge and a mint border. Future cards show the date label in the corner. Each card shows: meal emoji or image, meal name, kcal, and a Skip button. Tapping a card opens the Meal Detail bottom sheet for that specific meal.

Renewal Nudge

A "Renew →" button appears in the subscription banner when 20% or fewer of the plan's days remain (e.g. fewer than 2 days on a Week Plan, fewer than 5 days on a Month Plan). Tapping it opens the Plan Selection screen with the customer's previous plan tagged "Your Last Plan."

B6
Meal Detail
A detailed view of a specific meal showing nutritional information, the chef's description, and the skip action. Available in two layouts — a bottom sheet from the home screen and a full screen from the menu.
Content Shown
Meal image / emoji
Hero image area. If the admin uploaded a photo for the dish in the Meal Library, it is shown here. If not, the emoji selected by the admin appears on a colour-coded gradient background — orange for Executive Meal, green for Salad Meal.
Badges
"Highly Rated" badge appears when the meal has strong historical ratings. Meal type badge (Executive / Salad).
Date label
Shows which day this meal is scheduled for — e.g. "Sunday, Apr 7 · Executive Meal."
Meal names
English name in large Fraunces typeface. Arabic name shown directly below in a smaller weight.
Macros
Four pills showing: Calories (kcal) · Protein (g) · Carbs (g) · Fat (g). Values are set by admin in the Meal Library when the dish is added.
Chef's Note
Short description in the chef's voice. Shown in a lemon-coloured box with italic text. Set by admin when adding the dish. Example: "Slow-cooked for 4 hours with saffron rice and dried lime."
Key Ingredients
Shown in the full-screen view only. Small chip tags listing the main ingredients.
Action button
Single full-width button: "⏭ Skip this day." This is the only action. There is no second button — the salad add-on is not in v1. The button is subject to the 6 PM cutoff and skip limit rules.
Two Layouts
Bottom Sheet
Opened from home meal card
Slides up from the bottom. Dismiss by swiping down or tapping the dark backdrop. Returns to the Home screen on close.
Full Screen
Opened from a menu row tap
Full page view with a floating back button top-left. Includes key ingredients chips. The Skip button is sticky at the bottom so it remains accessible while the customer scrolls through the content.
B7
Menu
The 7-day menu view shows all meals for the current week. It is accessible from the bottom navigation and is intended to be viewable by all users regardless of subscription status.
Menu Structure
Filter tabs
Three tabs: All (default — shows both meal types per day), Executive (executive meals only), Salad (salad meals only). Tabs filter which rows are shown — they do not restrict which meals exist.
TODAY badge
The current day's section is labelled with a green "TODAY" badge next to the day name. The working week runs Sunday to Thursday.
Each row shows
Colour-coded meal thumbnail (orange = Executive, green = Salad) · English meal name · Arabic meal name · Calorie count · Chevron indicating it is tappable.
Tapping a row
Opens Meal Detail Full Screen for that meal. Past meals open in a read-only view — no skip action is available for past delivery days.
Data source
Menu content flows from what the admin published in the Menu Manager for that week. Once published, the customer app updates immediately.
Menu Accessibility — Intended Behaviour
✅
Menu is open to all users in all states
The Menu screen is designed to be fully browsable by all customers regardless of subscription status — Unsubscribed, Active, Expired, Paused, and Resumed. No meal should be locked or hidden at the menu level. The menu serves as a preview and an ongoing engagement tool for customers who are considering renewing or are between plans.
⚠️ Known Build Correction
🔧
Menu not accessible in Expired plan state — fix required
In the current built prototype, the Menu tab is not visible or accessible when a customer's plan is in the Expired state. This is a gap from the intended design. The production build must ensure the Menu screen is accessible in all subscription states, including Expired. A customer should always be able to browse this week's menu regardless of whether they have an active plan.
B8
Skip a Day
Customers can skip any scheduled delivery before the 6 PM cutoff the day before. There is no per-skip charge. The total number of skips available is bounded only by the 2× plan window rule.
How Skipping Works
Cutoff time
6 PM Riyadh time (AST, UTC+3) on the day before delivery. The Saudi working week is Sunday–Thursday, so:
· For a Sunday delivery: cutoff is Saturday 6 PM
· For a Monday delivery: cutoff is Sunday 6 PM
After the cutoff, the skip button is hidden or disabled for that meal.
What happens when skipped
The day is marked as skipped. No meal is produced or delivered for that customer on that day. The total number of meals in the plan does not increase to compensate — skipped meals are not replaced or rolled forward.
Undo skip
A skipped day can be un-skipped before the 6 PM cutoff. After the cutoff passes, the skip is final and cannot be reversed.
Skip limit
No fixed number of skips per week. However, all skips must occur within the 2× plan window (see A2). Once that window closes, the Skip button is disabled. The limit resets completely on renewal.
Skip Limit Reached
⚠️
What the customer sees when the limit is hit
A red banner appears at the top of the Skip a Day screen: "Skip limit reached for this subscription period" with a link to the FAQ explaining the 2× window rule. Individual skip buttons for affected days are greyed out and cannot be tapped. Skips that were already set before the limit was hit can still be undone if the 6 PM cutoff has not yet passed. The limit resets when the customer renews their subscription.
Where Skip Actions Are Available

Skip can be triggered from three places in the app:

1. Home screen meal cards — each card in the horizontal scroll has an inline "⏭ Skip" button below the meal details.

2. Meal Detail — both the bottom sheet and the full-screen view have a "⏭ Skip this day" button.

3. Skip a Day screen — accessed from My Plan tab → Skip a Day. Shows a full list of all scheduled delivery days with their status: Delivered / Skipped (with Undo option) / Available (with Skip button) / Limit Reached (button greyed).

B9
Pause Subscription
Customers can pause their subscription for a defined date range. No meals are delivered and no charge is made during a pause. The remaining plan days are frozen and restart when the customer resumes.
How Pausing Works
How to initiate
From the My Plan tab: tap the Pause Subscription toggle. This opens the Pause Modal — the subscription is not paused immediately. The pause is only applied when the customer confirms in the modal.
Pause Modal
A calendar date picker with two fields: Start Date and End Date. The customer selects the date range they want to pause. Dates beyond the 2× plan window ceiling are greyed out in the calendar and cannot be selected.
During the pause
Plan days are frozen — the count does not decrease. No meals are produced or delivered. No charge is made. The Home screen transitions to the purple Paused state showing: days frozen, paused-on date, and the resumes date.
Ceiling enforcement
The pause end date cannot extend beyond the 2× plan window. These dates are greyed out in the calendar picker. If a customer does not manually resume by the ceiling date, the system automatically resumes the subscription at the ceiling date.
Window reset
Like skip limits, the pause window resets completely when the customer renews. Unused pause capacity from one subscription period does not carry over to the next.
Resuming from Pause
Entry points
Two routes: (1) "Resume Subscription →" in the purple paused card on the Home screen. (2) "Resume Now" in the 1×3 quick actions row on the Paused Home state. Both open the Resume Confirmation Modal.
Resume options
Two choices in the modal: (1) "Resume from tomorrow" — the default option, shows the first delivery date. (2) "Choose a specific start date" — reveals a date picker to select any future working day (Sunday–Thursday).
What resumes
The remaining frozen days restart from the chosen date. The customer's meal type, delivery address, and all other preferences carry over unchanged. There is no charge to resume.
Resume Success screen
Purple gradient screen: "Welcome back, [Name]!" Shows plan, days remaining, first delivery date, and explicitly states "Charged today: Nothing — ₀" to confirm no payment was taken.
